# 01 — Project Constitution

## Mission Statement

Build the definitive digital home for **Saampark Group** — a unified, world-class web presence that clearly communicates the group's two subsidiaries, their offerings, and their value proposition to businesses across India and beyond.

> **Tagline:** *Aspire For Optimum Excellence*

---

## The Group Structure

Saampark Group operates through two legally distinct, complementary subsidiaries:

| Entity | Full Name | Short Name | ISO Certification | Focus |
|--------|-----------|------------|-------------------|-------|
| **STR** | Saampark Technology & Research Pvt. Ltd. | STR | ISO 9001:2015 | Website, Software & App Development |
| **SCS** | Saampark Consultancy Service | SCS | ISO 9001:2015 | Digital Marketing & Business Solutions |

Both entities are co-powered under the **Saampark Group** brand.

---

## Core Objectives

1. **Establish brand authority** — Present Saampark Group as a premium, trusted technology & consultancy group
2. **Generate qualified leads** — Drive inbound inquiries for both STR and SCS services
3. **Showcase capabilities** — Demonstrate expertise through structured service pages, case studies, and portfolios
4. **Enable self-service discovery** — Let visitors explore packages, pricing, and offerings without friction
5. **Build digital credibility** — Reinforce ISO certification, client trust signals, and professional identity

---

## Golden Rules

> **Before writing or modifying any code, read every document inside `/docs`. These documents are the permanent source of truth for the project. If code conflicts with the documentation, always follow the documentation.**

1. **Docs are law** — All architectural, design, and content decisions must align with these documents
2. **Brand consistency** — Every pixel must reflect the Saampark brand system defined in `02-brand-system.md`
3. **Mobile-first** — All layouts must be designed mobile-first; desktop is an enhancement
4. **Performance matters** — Target Lighthouse score ≥ 90 across all categories
5. **No lorem ipsum** — All content must be real, meaningful, and aligned with Saampark's actual services
6. **Accessibility** — Meet WCAG 2.1 AA standards across all pages
7. **SEO-ready from day one** — Every page must have proper meta tags, structured data, and semantic HTML

---

## Project Scope

### In Scope
- Saampark Group homepage (unified brand landing)
- STR subsidiary pages (web dev, app dev, software)
- SCS subsidiary pages (digital marketing, ads, legal/business services)
- Service detail pages with pricing
- About / Team / Contact pages
- Blog / Knowledge base (structure only at launch)
- Marketplace / packages explorer
- Admin-facing CMS hooks (future)

### Out of Scope (v1)
- Client portal / dashboard
- Payment gateway integration (v2)
- Multi-language support (v2)
- Native mobile apps
